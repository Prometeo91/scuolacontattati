Initiatic Test of the Four Elements

Hermetic assessment of the inner structure

General instructions

  • For each question: from 1 to 5 in sections 1–5, 7 and 8; from 1 to 10 in section 6 (Threefold Structure).
  • Sections 1–4 (qualities). Below 18 the Element is missing (imbalance by deficiency).
  • Minimum total for an Element to be considered present: ≥ 18.
  • Minimum total for an Element to be considered dominant: ≥ 24.
  • Section 5 (excess). Minimum total for a tendency to excess: ≥ 18.
  • Minimum total for an Element to be considered in excess: ≥ 24.
  • Minimum total for an Element to be considered out of balance: ≥ 30 (hermetic alarm threshold).
  • The two sections are read together: excess counts first, and high qualities do not offset it.

✦ 9. Initiatic interpretation

Elements: qualities (sections 1–4)

  • < 18 → Missing Element (imbalance by deficiency)
  • ≥ 18 → Present Element
  • ≥ 24 → Dominant Element

Elements: excess (section 5)

  • ≥ 18 → tendency to excess, to be observed
  • ≥ 24 → Element in excess, to be rebalanced
  • ≥ 30 → Element out of balance (to be transmuted)

State of each Element

  • qualities ≥ 18 and excess < 24 → in balance
  • qualities < 18 and excess < 24 → in deficiency
  • qualities ≥ 18 and excess ≥ 24 → in excess: the strength is there but degenerates
  • qualities < 18 and excess ≥ 24 → out of balance: the shadow of the Element without its quality
  • excess ≥ 30 → out of balance (to be transmuted)

Threefold Structure

  • ≥ 20 for each centre → Mature threefold structure
  • ≥ 24 → Integrated threefold structure
  • ≥ 27 → Operative I
  • ≥ 30 → Magical I

Attention

  • ≥ 24 → Unified attention
  • ≥ 27 → Magical attention
  • ≥ 30 → Continuity inside/outside (Netzach)

Will

  • ≥ 24 → Ordering will
  • ≥ 27 → Directional will
  • ≥ 30 → Hermetic will (magical act possible)

Test of your temperamental profile

The four temperaments of Hippocrates and Galen

Every human being is a microcosm in which all the elements of nature flow and dance.

For over two thousand years, medicine and psychology have studied human nature through one of the most fascinating insights in history: the theory of the four temperaments of Hippocrates and Galen.

Unlike modern personality tests, this view does not put you in a fixed "box". Ancient medicine knew well that none of us is made of a single pure element. Each of us has a unique combination, a personal hierarchy in which Air, Fire, Earth and Water (and their humours: blood, yellow bile, black bile and phlegm) coexist in different proportions.

This test is not meant to label you, but to draw your inner map. Through your emotional reactions, your everyday behaviour and the way you live your relationships, we will discover:

  1. Your predominant temperament (the element that guides your primary energy).
  2. The exact hierarchy of the other elements within you and their percentages, to understand how your nuances of character, your strengths and your areas of growth balance each other.

Before you begin: Make yourself comfortable, answer on instinct thinking of who you really are in everyday life (and not who you would like to be), and get ready to discover the secret chemistry of your personality.

How to take the test

Read the 60 statements, divided by area, and give each one a score from 1 to 5, according to how much it reflects your real life, your emotional reactions and your daily routine:

  • 1 = Not true at all / Does not represent me
  • 2 = Rarely true
  • 3 = Sometimes true / It depends
  • 4 = Often true / Represents me a lot
  • 5 = Completely true / That is exactly it

✦ How the hierarchy and percentages are calculated

  • Each area has 15 statements from 1 to 5: its score ranges from 15 to 75.
  • Total: the four scores are added together (Sanguine + Choleric + Melancholic + Phlegmatic) to find the Overall Score.
  • Percentage: for each temperament, its score is divided by the Overall Score and multiplied by 100.
