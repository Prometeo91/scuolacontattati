Enneagram Test: Typical Phrases

To recognise your essential Enneagram type

You tick every phrase that feels close to your way of being, thinking or reacting. There is no minimum or maximum. The 270 phrases are divided into nine sections, one per Enneagram type, each opening with the name of the profile and its central theme. At the end the test counts how many phrases you ticked for each profile: the type with the highest score is your primary, or essential, type. Another type within 1–3 points may be a wing or a second strong type.

Available in Italian for now

The phrases, written by the School’s guides, have not yet been translated, so the test is currently in Italian only. The page keeps your ticks on this device, so you can stop and continue whenever you like.
